# Pairwise dependency graphs: one edge type from an ordered-pair test
Slug: 2026-10-14-pairwise-dependency-graphs | From: plans/2026-10-13-object-level-rules-dependency-graphs.md

## Goal
The page's dependency graphs get one edge type computed from the goal load alone: A before B when only that order lands both dishes under the benchmark's move (3 mm release, Isaac settle), for the same 5 best restricted loads; the sequential build certifies each acyclic graph and reports how many constraints it had to add.

## Decisions (from grilling, 2026-10-14)
- One edge type; the orange (learned) and grey (neighbour) classes go. An edge A before B has one of three physical reasons, kept as a JSON attribute only: knock (A dropped last into the full goal load moves B > 10 mm or 20°, so the dropper precedes the moved dish), need (B lands within the at-goal tolerance with A present but not with A removed), block (A lands only with B removed). A two-way pair is a cycle: drawn as a double edge, the load captioned "no valid one-at-a-time order", no build. Rejected: knock and need only; two edge styles.
- Context: the other dishes at their jointly settled poses (the gate snapshot), re-settled before each drop, disturbance judged against the re-settled poses as the sequencer does. Rejected: the pair alone (blind to third dishes). This follows Ren and Qureshi (IROS 2024): graph over the goal, DAG, topological order, cycle = no order; their access tunnel is replaced by the settle test because the benchmark has no arm.
- Candidates: pairs whose collision pieces come within 20 mm at the goal poses (FCL, Kit-free), any rack; the rest are independent by geometry. Knock edges may point at any dish the drop moved, candidate or not. Rejected: 15 cm centre distance, every pair.
- Loads: r05_00014 r07_00023 r11_00050 r00_00031 r02_00048 with the existing gate dirs; r05 stays gate-rejected. Each test once, margins recorded. Rejected: repeating marginal tests, more loads.
- Certifier: `build_sequence` started from the graph's edges; extra constraints = learned minus graph. Captions print pairs tested, edges by reason, cycles, builds, extra constraints and S packed → settled → built.
- A load whose certifying build still fails after 8 builds keeps its complete graph; the caption names the failing dish, the reason and the extra constraints learned. Rejected: captioning it like a cycle.
- Run time (2026-10-14, after implementation): a drop costs 360 ticks with a 23-dish context, so a load takes 25 to 40 min; the four sessions run in parallel (about 40 min wall, 2 h deadline each), approved as is. Rejected: a 60-tick context settle.
- Page: the dependency section is replaced (definition, one style, new captions); everything else untouched; published as Version 17. The old three-class graphs stay in Version 16 and the run note. Rejected: keeping both sections.
- Caveat round (2026-10-14, decided without the user, who asked for the caveats to be worked on): (a) settle noise: the four loads are run a second time with identical inputs (`--out-dir pairs_rerun`) and the page reports edges reproduced per load; (b) sampler: restricted loads ranked 6 to 17 were joint-gated (11 of the top 17 accepted) and every accepted one gets a graph (`--out-dir pairs_more`, merged into the published set), the rejected ones a labelled row; (c) two-way loads: `--goal built` rebuilds the goal one dish at a time under the own-load rule (settle deviation <= 8 cm, contact < 2 mm, nothing placed disturbed), takes the built poses as the goal and reruns the pairwise test (`pairs_resettled/`), shown in a sub-section with the goal's displacement and S before and after. Not done: the gripper-swept prism (needs the arm), the planning ablation, the counter-cap feasibility check.
- Caveat round, after the runs (2026-10-14 10:25 UTC): the identical rerun reproduced every drop bit for bit (the settle is deterministic here), so the noise measure becomes a second run with the release perturbed by up to 3 mm laterally and 3° in yaw (`--perturb-mm 3 --perturb-deg 3 --perturb-seed 1 --out-dir pairs_perturbed`), reported as edges reproduced under the perturbation. The re-settle of r00_00031 and r02_00048 failed under the own-load rule too (the second bowl of each pair lands on the first with 8-9 mm contact and pushes it; the sequencer closes a two-cycle after 2 and 4 builds): those pairs must be placed together, stated on the page; no further fix this round.
- Perturbed-release result (2026-10-14 11:02 UTC): with the release offset by up to 3 mm and 3° (seed 1), 15 of 21 edges came back, 6 were lost and 5 appeared; r07_00023 turned into a cycle (plate_03 and plate_07) and r02_00048 lost its plate cycle, while every bowl cycle held. Contact failures are all-or-nothing at the millimetre level, so the page reports edges as release-sensitive; one seed only, more seeds are a later round.
- Version 19 (2026-10-14 14:06 UTC, user request after the window): the dependency graph of each of the five best restricted loads also appears under its still in "Loads in the Isaac scene" (the same PNG, one caption sentence with the graph's outcome, a lead sentence linking the dependency section); page only, `page_v19` from the v18 figures. The dependency section stays; the random and the five worst restricted loads have no graph, the graph loads of ranks 6-17 no still. Rejected: moving the section, rendering 12 more stills.
