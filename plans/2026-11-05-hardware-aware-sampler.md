# A hardware-aware goal: the sampler admits only poses this arm can place
Slug: 2026-11-05-hardware-aware-sampler | From: research/2026-11-05-hardware-aware-sampler.md, results/2026-11-05-fifteen-r3.md (12 of 15 placed; bowl_02, bowl_03 and cup_05 unplaceable by any release), plans/2026-11-02-fit-score-prove.md (the sampler this changes)

## Goal
For s0's roster (plate5-bowl5-cup5, allowance 3, seed 0) the sampler produces a goal load whose every dish the UR5e with the side-mounted 2F-85 at posture_v2 places by a direct release, and one robot run places 15 of 15.

## Decisions (the user, 2026-11-05 04:55 to 07:10 EST)
- New direction instead of per-dish release fixes: the goal sampler considers the hardware when sampling. Rejected the round-4 remedies (placing path, release lower, a curved path search) because they fix one dish at a time against a goal the hand was never meant to build.
- Admissible pose: the arm holds the dish at that pose with the open hand 2 mm clear of everything, with a pick and a carry (the Kit-free gate's G2); released where it rests, no drop, no slide, no insertion stop. Rejected "any release that works Kit-free" (the landings are unmodelled, as rounds 1 to 3 showed) and "reachable only" (the release is where the three dishes failed).
- The constraint enters lazily: CP-SAT packs as now, the access-order tool gates the load in dag order with the earlier dishes in the scene, each failure becomes a cut, the packer re-solves, until every dish passes a direct release; then the Isaac build, gate, certify and score as now. Rejected the pre-filter of every pose (the carry and the scene are not static) and a learned filter (no labels yet).
- Machine: the basket in. The sampler avoids the sites the hand cannot reach past it. Rejected basket out because the goal and S would describe a machine the benchmark does not use.
- Gate pose: the Kit-free loop gates each dish at the rest model's predicted landing (order.rest_entries; the packed pose where no rest table exists), and the Isaac-settled goal is gated once more after the build; a mismatch costs one more iteration with an Isaac build. Rejected the packed pose alone (the settle moved s0's dishes up to 44 mm) and Isaac in every iteration (10 box minutes each).
- Cuts by the collision set, shrunk before they are added: a pose that fails alone is banned (the packer's bans, by cand_key, which the ascent also obeys); a pose that fails only beside one named earlier dish becomes a pair no-good after a deletion filter capped at 3 gate calls confirms the culprit; otherwise a no-good on the whole load. A carry RRT timeout is retractable: retried once at the 180 s budget before it counts. Rejected unary bans only (over-forbids) and whole-load no-goods only (too weak).
- No score ascent this round; the fit alone picks the load. The result is a new sample id <s0 id>__hw with its own S_ref, reported beside s0's 0.513; s0 and the benchmark stay untouched. Rejected the ascent with bans only (its moves are not gated); a gated ascent is a later round.
- Where and how much: the loop runs Kit-free on the Mac (6 workers), at most 6 iterations, else the roster is reported infeasible for this hand; then one 2 h box window (kill at T0 + 108, stop at T0 + 116) runs the Isaac stages of the new sample, the access order on the settled goal, and the full robot chain with the video, started on the user's go.
- The plan grilling (07:20 EST): the __hw sample reuses s0's metadata and initial blocks (the start depends on the roster and the seed, not the goal; the ledger records the copy); no packing under the cuts at level 0 tries the level 1 families under the same cuts, and still none means the roster is reported infeasible for this hand at this mount with the cut history (no relaxed solve: the goal must hold all 15); the direct moves are exactly four: bowls D-ring or D-ring-side at the goal pose, cups D-side-down at the goal pose, plates I4-wall20 released at the seat (release.mode seat, seat at goal), and inserts and drops are not offered to the gate; the workflow starts now, the Mac loop runs as soon as the checker passes, the box window waits for the user's go.

## Change (one workflow: two implementers, one checker; Opus, max effort)
- A, the loop: code/src/bench/build.py (and a new code/src/bench/hwgate.py): between plan_goal and the Kit build, write the candidate load as a four-block JSON (metadata.sample_id, goal.slots, goal.poses at the rest-model poses, dag.order and edges from order.dag_order), call the access-order tool by subprocess with --mount data/results/robot/mount/posture_v2.json, no insert sites, no state dirs, the basket in, read order.json, apply the direct-release predicate per dish (pass; move in D-ring, D-ring-side, D-side-down, I4-wall20; no build_release, no stop_mode; a plate's release.mode seat with seat_at_goal ok; release_check ok), map each failure to its cut (cand_key -> bans; a named culprit -> the deletion filter -> a pair no-good; else a load no-good), re-solve; stop at all pass or 6 iterations; record metadata.provenance.hwgate (iterations, cuts by type, gate seconds, the mount, basket in) and goal.robot (per dish move, hold, spin). A flag `--hardware posture_v2.json` on bench.py selects it and the __hw sample id; the stages after build run unchanged, plus the re-gate after build.
- B, the robot tools: frigidaire_access_order.py takes the candidate JSON, a `--no-insert-sites` and `--direct-only` mode (no insert or drop moves offered), and writes the culprits per failing dish; frigidaire_placing_loads.py --fifteen reads goal.robot so the chain uses the loop's moves and spins; the gate's pass for D-ring-side and D-side-down includes release_check.ok.
- Checker (Mac, Kit-free): pytest unchanged; one loop on s0's roster at seed 0 with the iteration log; the four-block JSON validates against the schema; the fake-Kit chain on the predicted goal. Docs: robot.md and the bench README get a dated paragraph. No new tests.
