# Drop test: carry the held dish above its goal, open the gripper, see if it settles (Part B, variant D)
Slug: 2026-10-27-drop-test | From: plans/2026-10-26-placing-goal.md, results/2026-10-26-side-pinch.md

## Goal
For each dish of the three single-kind easy loads, with the dish welded in hand at its counter hold, the arm reaches the lowest collision-free pose straight above the dish's goal, opens the gripper, and the page says whether the dropped dish settled and where it landed, with one replay per load and the goal arrangement as a still.

## Decisions (from grilling, 2026-10-27 01:45-02:25 EDT)
- Drop pose = the counter hold kept (dish mouth-up as picked: bowl rim pinch pivoted 21 deg, cup wall pinch, plate assumed rim hold at round 1's 38 deg pivot), TCP pointing down, dish centroid over the goal centroid's x,y; the lowest collision-free IK scanning 25 to 350 mm above the goal centroid in 25 mm steps (25 mm floor, user: gentler drops) (arm model with the held dish, racks in their state, earlier dishes where they lie). Carry by the existing transit (safe height, above, down). Rejected the goal orientation (W: 0/23) and one fixed height.
- Success = settled at rest by the benchmark's settle (bench_settle: 150 ticks, drift <= 5 mm / 3 deg over the last 60); nothing else gates it. Recorded as information per dish: drop height, where it landed (rack, tub floor, counter, on another dish; from the contact pairs at rest), centroid distance and tilt from the goal, mouth up or down, earlier dishes that moved (information only: a knocked earlier dish never fails the drop). Rejected "in a rack only", "within 50 mm" and failing on a knock.
- Sequence = the witness order; earlier dishes stay where they settled, failed ones too; a dish with no collision-free drop pose or no path is "no reach", skipped, the load goes on. Rack state per dish as today (upper rack in for a lower-rack goal, lower rack in for an upper-rack goal). Rejected resetting earlier dishes to their goals and stopping at the first failure.
- Plates run with the assumed rim hold (no plate pick exists); the page says so.
- Page: Part B becomes the drop test only; the W and S tables leave the page (one sentence points to results/2026-10-26-placing-goal.md and -side-pinch.md; records stay). Per load: the goal-arrangement still beside the as-landed still (the replay scene, same camera), one replay of the whole load in order, the per-dish table. Rejected keeping W and S below.
- Run tonight to completion (user, 02:25 EDT, start now): three loads in parallel, one Kit process per load on its own physical core, box off right after the copy-back.
- Outcome (box 02:41-03:24 EDT, 42 billed min; results/2026-10-27-drop-test.md): settled 9/23 (bowls 2/7, plates 0/8, cups 7/8), no reach 14 (7 no collision-free IK at any height or yaw, 7 IK but no RRT path, the plate retry at 45 s included); every settled dish lies mouth up except bowl_02, 70-172 deg from its mouth-down goal; no earlier dish knocked. Bowl rows from the legacy-tolerance run (session decision A: the 0.044 rad carried-bowl wrist lag is the R7 controller artifact, not a physical failure; the default-tolerance rows stay as data).
