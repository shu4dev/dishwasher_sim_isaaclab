## Goal
One run of the robot pipeline grasps and places all 15 dishes of s0 from the counter, in two rack chains with the placed dishes staying, judged seated and at goal by the rerun's rules, with one stitched video. The pipeline is general in its parts: per-kind holds, a per-hold drive command, a mount found by a reach search, an order and approach chosen with the arm's access, a guarded insertion at every site.
