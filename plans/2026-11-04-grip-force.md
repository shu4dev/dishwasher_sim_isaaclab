# Grip force: calibrate the finger drives to the real hand's squeeze, re-gate the side ring hold, place bowl_04
Slug: 2026-11-04-grip-force | From: results/2026-11-04-ring-side-hold.md (bowl_04 turned 38 and 50 deg in the pads about the closing axis at 1.8 to 2.2 N per pad), with results/2026-11-04-s0-rerun.md (the pad force measurement: the close peaks at 83 to 85 N, the steady squeeze is 1.4 to 3.5 N; the real 2F-85 grips at 20 to 235 N)

## Goal
The pads hold the mouth-down bowl's foot ring with a steady squeeze of 20 N or more per pad (the real 2F-85's minimum), found by a measured sweep of the finger drives in the grip probe, and at that drive the side ring hold still passes its Isaac gate and bowl_04 is placed at upper_bowl_11 (then bowl_02 at lower_frontright), each bowl alone in the scene.

## Decisions (the user, 2026-11-04 14:00 EST grilling, and the coordinator)
- The target: a steady 20 N or more per pad on the ring over a 5 s hold, measured by the PhysX contact reports as the pad_force.json figures were. The lowest passing drive values win (the ones nearest today's 8 N m, stiffness 400, damping 1.0; inner finger drives effort 10, stiffness 10, damping 0.05).
- The knobs: the finger drives only. finger_joint effort limit, stiffness and damping, and the inner finger drive gains (ur5e.robot_cfg, flags R2). The close target 0.8 rad, the mimic coupling, the pad friction 1.0 and torsional radius, every margin, gate limit and fault knob stay. Nothing may exceed the real hand: a pad above 235 N is the harness violation it already is.
- The sweep, in the grip probe (frigidaire_grip_probe.py --inverted --foot-clamp: the bowl mouth down, the pads on the foot ring, the support drops 10 cm, the bowl hangs 3 s), one Kit process per setting on the box: first today's setting, to reproduce the 2 N steady squeeze and read what limits it from the logged applied torque and the inner finger angles (the close peaks at 85 N, so the drive can push: the loss is in the steady state); then at most 8 settings in the order that reading suggests (the inner finger stiffness first if those joints yield, the effort limit 8 to 16 to 32 N m if the drive saturates, damping last), each judged on the steady pad force, the slip under 5 mm over the hang and no violation. The probe's own bowl is the shipped HOTEC bowl.
- Then, at the chosen drive: the side ring hold's Isaac gate (10 trials, 5 s hold, pass at 8 or more held, the pad force reported), bowl_04 try c (the straight-first start order, --ring-side-order straight) and bowl_02, each alone at the spot, racks as s0's goal frames, mount posture_v1, the sensor-stopped release; the cup side-down and plate wall gates (10 trials each) at the end if the window has time, since the drive is shared. A gate under 8 held ends the placements and reports the sweep.
- Before the window (the coordinator, 14:30 EST): bowl_02 is skipped in Isaac and written as a reject row, because it has no Kit-free carry path with either hold (the side hold none at 300 s, the axial hold none under the tilt cap) and the drive changes no geometry; bowl_01 with the side ring hold takes its place as the second placement (G2 Kit-free with straight legs, goal tilt 54.9 deg, placed by the axial hold in the rerun: a comparison of the calibrated hold at a second site). The cup side-down and plate wall gates run alongside the side ring gate on their own cores instead of after the placements.
- Mid-window (the sweep, 14:50 EST; the user, 15:00 EST): the steady squeeze collapses because PhysX's finger_joint drive hits its 1.0 rad/s joint velocity limit at the ring's 0.57 rad error and then delivers about 0.17 N m; the drive keeps k x error only while k x err / (c + k dt) stays under the limit, so c >= 0.57 k, and the inner finger joints have no PhysX drive at all. 8/4/2.5 gives 22.5 N steady (8/5/5 27.5 N, 8/6/4 33 N) at the cost of a slow close that never reaches the plate wall at 0.76 rad inside the rig's 1 s close. Decided: flags R2 stay unchanged this window (the placements run at 8/4/2.5 by override, recorded per row); no velocity-limit probe (a new knob, for a grilled round, capped by the real hand's closing speed of about 1.4 rad/s); the next round makes the drive a per-hold command (bowls and cups 8/4/2.5, plates 8/400/1), as the real 2F-85 takes force and speed per grip command.
- The new drive values become the flags' R2 defaults only if the side ring gate passes; otherwise the flags stay and the note carries the sweep table. Cap 1.5 h from T0 (RUNNING) to STOPPED: kill at T0 + 78 min, stop at T0 + 86 min whatever the state.
