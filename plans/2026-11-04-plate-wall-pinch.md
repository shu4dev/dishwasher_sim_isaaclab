# Plate pick: pinch the wall along its normal
Slug: 2026-11-04-plate-wall-pinch | From: plans/2026-11-04-plate-pick-presentation.md (the edge pinch, held 0 of 10 in the three-fixes window, results/2026-11-04-three-fixes.md)

## Goal
The 2F-85 holds s0's plate from the counter spot in 8 of 10 Isaac trials with a pinch whose pads lie flat on the plate's wall, so the I4 descent at plate_05, plate_03 and plate_04 has a real pick to start from.

## Decisions (from the measurements; the user is away, so these are the session's)
- Why both measured pinches fail: the HOTEC plate is a coupe (rim 228.6 mm, 40 mm tall, wall 3.5 mm, foot 140 mm, a 6 mm bulge), so its wall climbs at about 40 deg from the foot to a 1 mm rim flat. The rim pinch closes radially and the edge pinch closes along the axis; both meet that wall at about 40 deg, so part of the closing force runs along the wall. Isaac measured both: the rim pinch held 3 of 10 and pivoted 38 deg; the edge pinch held 0 of 10, the hand pushed 25 to 32 mm outward during the close. Rejected a third axis-aligned pinch, and a clamp fixture (it would hold the plate during the close while the pads still shear along the wall).
- The hold: the closing axis along the wall's local normal at the pinch point, the approach along the wall from outside the rim edge, the pads' centre 20 mm below the rim edge along the wall, both rolls of the hand about the approach as candidates, 12 yaws round the rim. The local normal comes from the asset's profile (hotec_gen, parameters.json), never typed. Rejected a deeper pinch (the pad reaches the well's floor) and the rim flat (1 mm wide).
- Presentations: the plate face up at the spot (no fixture) and leaning 30 deg on the 100 mm backstop, both swept Kit-free under the phase 1 rules (the arm and the gripper at the 15 mm margin, the held plate under the 1 mm rule, G1 and G2); the I4 check at plate_05, plate_03 and plate_04 (plate_04 with the site sweep's 30 deg spin) with the new hold and D-seat's 2 mm rule on the final descent. The Isaac gate (10 trials, partA_plate_wall20) runs on the presentation with the most passing yaws among those whose I4 passes at least one goal; under 8 held, the plates stay reject rows in the s0 rerun.
- Phase 1 result (06:35 EST, frigidaire_plate_wall_pinch.py, 296 s): face up at 20 mm picks at 3 of 12 yaws (5 to 7, where the pinch hangs past the counter edge; pivot 0.1026 N m, 98 percent of a's); leaning 30 deg picks at 0 of 12 (the held plate against wrist 1 at the lift). I4 with the face-up pick passes no goal: roll 180 reaches the 50 mm descent start at plate_05, plate_03 and plate_04 (G0 and G1 pass), but leg 1 never completes, because the plate must turn 128 to 131 deg from the pick to the goal, the wrist 238 to 258 deg, and the held plate meets wrist 1 half way (RRT-Connect 45 s finds nothing). The wall slopes 48.6 deg at the pinch; over the 38 mm pad the arc's sagitta is 2.42 mm, so the pads close to 6.5 mm on three contacts (the outer pad at its centre, the inner pad at both ends), not on a flat 3.5 mm wall. Gate hulls are 1.3 mm per pad tighter than that close, so the descent check is slightly optimistic.
- Amendment, phase 1b: the pick presentation is the goal orientation itself. The plate stands on its rim edge at the spot, leaning 32 deg as every s0 plate goal does (rack-local quaternion xyzw 0, 0.8746, 0, 0.4848, so its face points toward the robot's side and down), its mouth face resting on the 100 mm backstop's top edge on the side it leans toward; the facing about world z is swept in 30 deg steps (12 facings, the goal's own among them), the pinch at the rim point highest at the goal (spin 0, with 30 and -30 as candidates), both rolls, the same hold. Leg 1 is then a translation with little wrist travel. Phase 1b result (07:20 EST, frigidaire_plate_goal_lean.py, 315 s): picks pass 36 of 72 tuples, I4 passes 27 of 108; four facings reach all three goals with roll 180 and spin 0 (330, 300 and 0 with straight leg 1 at 376, 379 and 386 deg of joint travel, 270 by RRT), the pads carry 0.0654 N m (63 percent of the flat pick's), the open gripper stays 32.7 mm from the plate on the approach. Chosen for the rerun: facing 0 (the goal orientation itself), roll 180, spin 0, over the smallest joint move at facing 330, because the plate then turns 0.3 to 2.4 deg between the pick and its goals against 30 deg, and a constant weight direction on the pads is this presentation's purpose. Corrections: plate_01 leans 28 deg, not 32 (it is a reject row anyway); leg 1 is straight but wrist 1 travels 112 to 148 deg; the mouth faces the backstop, so the box's top corner sits 1 mm inside the well at r 81.7 mm, z 10.5 mm. Isaac gate partA_plate_wall20 in the rerun window (07:28 EST): held 5 of 10, and every pinch that was reached held (jaws 0.76 rad, pivot under 0.51 deg, drift under 0.69 mm over 5 s); the five misses fell off the backstop during the spawn settle (91 to 95 deg in four trials, 60 deg in one), and even the held trials rested 29 to 34 mm and 23 to 24 deg off the planned pose. The hold is right, the two-contact presentation is not static. Phase 1c (decided 07:29 EST): the plate lies on a tilted plank with a lip under its bottom rim, the same orientation and hold (frigidaire_plate_goal_plank.py, outputs <s0>__fixes/plates_wall3/); the rerun window re-gates it on the idle lower cores only if the fixture passes Kit-free before T0 + 95 min, else the plates get their own window. Phase 1c result (07:30 EST, 8 s): a plank under the foot cannot hold the plate (in the goal orientation the foot faces up 32 deg), so the plate lies mouth down on a plank inclined 58 deg (240 x 150 x 20 mm, top face in the rim plane; 170 and 190 mm long planks meet the open gripper) with a 1 mm lip under the bottom rim; 0.530 g presses the plate onto the plank, 0.848 g onto the lip, the weight line 63.8 mm inside the support polygon; plate and fixture sit 22.8 mm higher. Facing 0, roll 180, spin 0 passes the pick and I4 at all three goals (G2, 50 mm starts, straight leg 1, turns 0.3 to 2.4 deg); facing 330 passes too with 13 deg less joint travel and 30 deg turns; facing 0 chosen.
