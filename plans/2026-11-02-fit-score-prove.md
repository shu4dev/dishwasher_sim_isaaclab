# The goal maker becomes fit, then score, then prove: first-fit packing with a clear descent, score ascent, DAG build order
Slug: 2026-11-02-fit-score-prove | From: results/2026-11-01-rack-wheels-visual.md, plans/2026-11-01-merged-benchmark.md (its score-greedy goal decision is superseded here)

## Goal
Every roster packs: the build stage produces a complete, insertable load with an ascent-improved S for easy, medium and hard, and the three rosters run through report on 6.2 in one window.

## Decisions (from grilling)
- The goal is the best load the score ascent reaches from a feasible packing of the roster, replacing the score-greedy (0 of 3 hard packings completed, 1 of 4 medium built: it takes the best gain with no view of capacity or of the insertion path). Rejected a capacity-aware greedy (S_ref stays greedy level, a first-fit probe per candidate), a CP-SAT assignment (a new dependency, about a week) and patches to the greedy (no capacity guarantee for hard).
- Fit: first-fit in the seed-shuffled family order, scarcest kind first (cups 8 rungs, bowls 13 sites, plates 19 gaps), each pose free by the packer's rules (0.8 mm touch, one plate or cup per gap, gate bans, nesting allowed) and with a clear descent past the dishes placed so far (the Reposer's Kit-free test); a dead end restarts with the next shuffle, up to 20.
- Score: the ascent ships in this round (rejected packing-only: a first-fit S_ref is a weak reference that track B beats routinely): `ascend` with the gain scorer at N/16 rays, 2 sweeps, the top 6 alternatives by isolated exposure plus 2 random per dish; a move is kept only when S rises and the load's access and support DAG (packed model) stays acyclic.
- Prove: the DAG's topological order is the build order (the dependency kit's `--order`, strict false: re-posing on a physical miss, deferral allowed), then the joint gate, certify, score (S_ref at full rays) and graph as today. Rejected the descent test alone (the ascent's moves can break the order) and leaving insertion to Isaac (the medium failures).
- Plan grill: a dead end in the fit restarts with the next seed-derived shuffle, up to 20 (rejected backtracking: more code for tight packings the restarts also find); all three rosters are sampled fresh into `data/results/bench/v3` under the one definition, v2 stays as history (rejected medium and hard into v2 beside the greedy easy pair); one window with a 3 h cap, the three rosters in parallel on pinned workers (rejected 4 h and builds-only); the ascent runs the DAG check only before committing a move that raises S (rejected checking every scored alternative: hundreds of 10 to 30 s checks per sweep).
- Revision after the Mac proof (2026-11-02): the plan's first-fit packed 0 of 80 medium and hard shuffles, and the cause is the upper bowl family, not the travel: the retraction sweep bans no upper pose at either travel, while the closed check against the upper rack's own tines (6 to 34 mm penetration) leaves 28 poses in 7 slots for the v4 bowl (29 poses in 10 slots for the October bowl; the family's coarse tilt and offset grid fits one pose per slot, and v4 lost slots 03, 10 and 11). Decided: (1) resample the upper bowl family for v4 (tilt every 5 deg, offset every 2.5 mm, small x shifts; the same closed check and sweep; the caches rebuild by digest) and recount the free poses and the compatible slot pairs; (2) the fit is an exact CP-SAT packing (one pose per dish over the masked candidates, the lower zones discretized to a 2 cm grid per tilt, pairwise collision conflicts from FCL, gate bans, DAG cycles cut lazily, isolated exposure as the tiebreak; it reports the max bowl count per level) with `pip install ortools` in the Mac venv and the container plus a Dockerfile line (approved); (3) if level 0 still holds fewer than 7 bowls beside 8 plates, level 1 admits the lower_mid zone (the 2026-09-29 ladder) and the pair records its level; (4) the approved 3 h window runs once medium and hard pack on 3 of 4 seeds on the Mac. Rejected: shrinking the rosters, a budgeted DFS, heuristic first-fit, an exact fit on today's families, more shuffles.
