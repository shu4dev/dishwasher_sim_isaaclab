## Goal
The shipped HOTEC bowl carries the user's measured foot ring (65.72 mm outer diameter, 3.16 mm wide, 5.57 mm tall, bowl 73.12 mm tall); on it, with the bowl mouth-down on the counter, the Part A gate says whether the 2F-85 holds the ring from outside (F-out) and from inside (F-in), and a Kit-free table says, for each measured hold, at which bowl goals and sampler release poses the arm reaches with the hand clear and above the bowl.
