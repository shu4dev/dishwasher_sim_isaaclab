**How the columns are defined:**
- **Verdict:** any of 12 spins passing ring_mount/rm.py's hand check at the goal (posture_v1 mount, ring hold, the loaded rack out and the other in).
- **Signed clearance:** the smallest FCL distance of the gripper, wrist 3 and links to the scene at the reported spin. When touching, it is minus the deepest contact.
- **Hand above:** TCP height over the bowl centroid.
- **Bowl path:** the held bowl's deepest penetration over its own 50 mm descent. It is a note, not a criterion, and is clear at 1 mm or less.
