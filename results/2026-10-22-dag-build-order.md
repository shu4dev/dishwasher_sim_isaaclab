- **Why, the main finding: at the packing's own poses the DAG is cyclic for every medium and hard packing.** All 83 medium and 91 hard draws that packed had a cycle. The reported cycles were plate-plate 2-cycles (28 / 28), bowl-bowl 2-cycles (28 / 33), bowl-plate 2-cycles (25 / 21), and a few 3-cycles. The rest of the 10 draws hit "no free pose". Packed poses are not where dishes land. A packed plate pivots about its centre (the lean variants rotate about the mid-height point over the gap), so its lower half overhangs the next gap on the side opposite its lean. That gives the reversed edge to that neighbour, while the settled plate pivots about its foot. Packed bowls sit 0-60 mm above their rest (the `lift` variants), and the builds' settle deviations reach 119 mm, so neighbouring packed bowls cover each other's descents both ways.
