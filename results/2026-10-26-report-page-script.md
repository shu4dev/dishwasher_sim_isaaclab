# Report page script: Hardware Limits Report (2026-10-26)
Plans: plans/2026-10-26-hardware-exhaustive.md (Decisions "Report") and plans/2026-10-26-placing-goal.md (Parts A and B); Mac only, Kit-free, no box time.
- What it does: `code/execution/frigidaire/frigidaire_robot_report_page.py` opens with two summaries. "Grasping: summary" (Part A) has one row per object and grasp from the records (held k/n, median pivot, carried_contact k/n plus "knuckle contact measured real" for the kind the knuckle check ran on (the bowl, from the verdict's trial path); the cup gets "the rim rests on the knuckles and fingers (contact check done on the bowl only)" in both Part A and its lead; source run); the cup's main row is the wall pinch from placing_v1/partA_cup_wall ("not run" until it lands), above "rim pinch (earlier; re-test after round 3)" and a generated grid footnote. "Placing the goal, dish already in hand" (Part B, from `--placing`/`--placing-media`, defaulting to placing_v1 beside the first records/media folder) gives, per load, a lead (the hold from the `grasp` field, e.g. "in hand by a wall pinch", omitted when absent; W k/n, R k/n, R-ideal k/n, subset stands), a step legend, a table with one row per dish in order (W, R and "R-ideal (flip assumed)": first failing step and why, or placed with its three errors, plus the runner's note; "not needed" when W placed it; disturbed), one pass and one fail clip, and a failing-step histogram (W, R and R-ideal counts). A missing load or file reads "not run". Then one section per object. Each opens with a generated lead (G3 held at the counter spot and over the grid; the knuckle phrase when carried_contact fired in every held trial; the sites at 9/10 or better and at 0/n; the Isaac tilt limit range). Then come grasp per counter spot; Kit-free reach per site and target (including `inhand.jsonl`); Isaac G4 per site and release mode (contact releases pooled; yaw retries as "released in the air, other yaws (yaw k)"; in-hand plates); a rack map; Rotation, with the opposite-tilt sentence, k/n per tilt (round 3 pooled per (site, tilt), each folder's share listed in a pooled cell), the Isaac limit as the first 0/n from 15° (the window-2 climb; round 3's 0° cells are listed as "(0°, round 3)" and never set it) or "above X° (none reached)", and a Kit-free limit over positive tilts only; then videos (every success clip of the window folders plus one failure, a physical failure ranked before a planner refusal), stills and failure modes, with captions and success/failure from the structured `videos.json` (an IK-branch-jump failure is marked as the planner's limit); Verdicts (knuckle, tines, seed-free reach, yaw retry, plates in hand); a byline with both windows; Sources. tilt_limits only cross-checks the rows: a non-numeric limit counts as "no Isaac limit", and any difference prints an `[INFO]` line. Rows skipped: limits_v1 `gate_*`, `w1_knuckle_dump`, r2's right-slot cup releases, and `_`-prefixed run folders. Identical clips are shown once.
- Command: `/opt/anaconda3/bin/python3 code/execution/frigidaire/frigidaire_robot_report_page.py --records data/results/robot/limits_v1 data/results/robot/exhaustive_w1 data/results/robot/exhaustive_w2 --media data/media/robot/limits_v1 data/media/robot/exhaustive_w1 data/media/robot/exhaustive_w2 --out data/media/robot/report_v1`.
