# HOTEC wheat-straw dinnerware assets — plate, bowl, cup (v1)

Three Isaac Sim USD assets of the user's HOTEC wheat-straw set, generated by
`code/src/dishsim/hotec_gen.py` into `data/assets/models/hotec_wheatstraw/v1/` (massless, 2026-09-21) and `v2/`
(the same geometry with the measured masses authored, 2026-09-22; `plate.usda`, `bowl.usda`,
`cup.usda`, `parameters.json`, `catalog.json`, `figures/`, `README.md`). Only this document and the
generator are tracked; the binaries live on the 2 TB drive behind the `data/assets/` symlink and are not
yet in the public archive (`archive_assets.py --kinds models` picks the directory up automatically on
a future upload).

Metres, `metersPerUnit = 1.0`, Z up. Origin at the **base centre**: z = 0 is the foot plane, the
opening faces +Z, width and depth are horizontal, height runs from the foot to the rim top. Default
prims `/HotecPlate`, `/HotecBowl`, `/HotecCup`; no xform ops anywhere.

**No prior HOTEC asset existed.** The request said "update my existing HOTEC assets"; a search of
the repo, its data roots and all 38 commits found no HOTEC or wheat-straw asset (the benchmark props
are YCB-derived, the Frigidaire tableware is a procedural catalog). v1 is therefore the first
version; each rebuild goes to a new folder and never overwrites an earlier one (the generator refuses to overwrite): `v2/` is v1 plus the measured masses.

**Scope.** Standalone assets with their own collision geometry, plus one settled load in the
Frigidaire FDPC4221AS twin for pictures and a video. They are not wired into the Bosch benchmark or
the Frigidaire catalog; a `--origin centre` build switch exists for the benchmark's centre-origin
convention.

## Model details

- **Parametric lathe.** Each shape is a 2-D cross-section from named physical parameters (below):
  foot ring and recess, a circular-arc side (plate, bowl) or a straight taper (cup), the exact
  inward offset of that curve as the interior wall, a flat cavity floor where the offset dips below
  the floor plane, and a rounded rim (quarter ellipse, top flat ≥ 0.6 mm). The bowl is explicitly
  **not a hemisphere**: its side is an arc of 8 mm sagitta from the foot edge to the rim, widest at
  the rim, and the flat floor radius is derived from the wall offset.
- **Visual mesh.** 96 vertices per ring, ~40–60 rows, vertex normals, watertight and consistently
  wound (checked). Bowl grooves (three, 0.6 mm deep) and cup facets (12, 0.6 mm deep, fading
  below the lip and above the base) are modelled on the **outer surface only**; the interior stays
  axisymmetric. Rings for the newer cup are a parameter (`cup.rings.heights_m`).
- **Collision.** One convex hull per control-row band per 15° sector (mid-angle vertices), the
  pattern of the Frigidaire tableware: 8 bands × 24 = 192 pieces (plate, bowl), 7 × 24 = 168 (cup).
  The control rows are a subset of the visual rows, so the collider never reaches outside the crest
  of the visual surface; its deepest intrusion into the cavity is 0.59 / 0.85 / 0.07 mm. Cavities,
  foot recess and openings are open (probed). Decorations are not in the collider.
- **Physics.** `PhysicsRigidBodyAPI` + `PhysxRigidBodyAPI` (16/4 solver iterations, CCD,
  max depenetration 0.5 m/s), `CollisionAPI` + `convexHull` on every piece (contact offset 0.5 mm),
  a friction material (0.45 / 0.35, restitution 0, estimated). **Mass:** v1 carries none (no
  `PhysicsMassAPI`, no `physics:density`). v2 carries the measured average of eight pieces on a
  kitchen scale (2026-09-22: 8 plates 757 g, 8 bowls 539 g, 8 cups 476 g) as `physics:mass` on the
  default prim only: plate 0.094625 kg, bowl 0.067375 kg, cup 0.0595 kg (`--set kind.mass_kg`,
  tagged `measured`); no density, centre of mass or inertia are authored, PhysX derives them from
  the collision shells. The PhysX-derived masses of the massless v1 (plate 0.210, bowl 0.126,
  cup 0.060 kg) overstated the plate by 2.2x and the bowl by 1.9x.
- **Provenance stamps.** Every file carries the generator's sha256 and the resolved parameter table
  (as one JSON string) plus its sha256 in the layer's `customLayerData`; `parameters.json` and
  `catalog.json` carry the same hash and the sha256 of every written file.

## Capacity

Brimful = volume of revolution of the delivered inner profile from the cavity floor to the
**lowest rim point**, closed by a temporary horizontal plane inside the calculation only (the
assets are not capped; a test asserts no triangle spans the rim plane). The rim is level because
the facets fade to zero before the lip: lowest rim point = height (0.0750 / 0.1049 m). The mesh
cross-check closes the delivered inner surface with a fan and takes its signed volume; the ratio
0.99929 is the 96-gon area factor. No plate target (its well holds ≈ 1.0 L to the rim).

**The cup mismatch.** 355 mL cannot be reached inside a 71.1 mm × 104.9 mm envelope with the
taper seen in the photos: over the 100.9 mm inner height it needs an inner radius ≥ 33.5 mm all the
way down, i.e. walls ≤ 2.1 mm **and** no taper (a 1.5 mm-walled straight cup gives 367 mL; the
zero-wall outer envelope with the 56 mm base holds 334 mL). Either the listing's width is not the
rim of the fluted cup, the height belongs to the other cup, or "12 oz" is a nominal size. The
profile was not distorted to close the gap.

## Measurements that resolve the estimates

- brimful water mass of one bowl and one cup (kitchen scale, ±1 g, ~20 °C water: 1 g ≈ 1 mL)
- DONE 2026-09-22: mass of each piece (8-piece totals 757 / 539 / 476 g, averaged; authored in v2)
- rim outer diameter, foot or base outer diameter, overall height of each kind (calipers/ruler)
- wall thickness at the rim and floor thickness (calipers)
- cup: number of facets (or rings), their depth, whether they reach the rim; which pack the cup is from
- bowl: heights of the three grooves above the foot plane

Rebuild with overrides, e.g. `--set cup.base_diameter_m=0.060 --set cup.wall_m=0.0022
--set cup.flutes.count=16`; overrides are tagged `measured` and change the parameter hash.
